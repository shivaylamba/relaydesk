# Support triage playbook
Collect the affected flow, approximate timestamp, browser and non-sensitive reference ID. Do not request passwords, card numbers, access tokens or personal financial records. For potential duplicate charges, ask whether the bank shows pending authorizations or settled transactions. If no approved incident resolution exists, collect the missing details and request internal triage. Do not assert that engineering is investigating before that work has actually begun. Do not invent a workaround or fix date.
